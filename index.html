Common questions

Do I have to sing? No. Singing is optional; most exercises are listening-only.

Why does the app ask for the microphone? Only for singing exercises, to detect your pitch on the device. Nothing is recorded.

I bought Pro on another device. Open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchase".

Best results: use headphones so reference tones do not leak into the microphone.
