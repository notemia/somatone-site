Somatone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Somatone is an ear-training app. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Somatone has no accounts, no servers and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect, transmit or sell any personal data and we do not track you.

Microphone

If you choose to sing, the microphone is used only while a singing exercise is active, to detect pitch on your device in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Listening-only use needs no microphone access, and you can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, chosen reference note and per-exercise results (cents and correct/incorrect summaries) are stored locally in the app. They may be included in your own device or iCloud backups according to your device settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Somatone does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
